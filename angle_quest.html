Module 1

Acute Angles

Select an angle below then click Let's Draw!

Hello Explorer! 👋

An Acute angle is any angle greater than 0° but less than 90°. Think of it as a sharp and pointy angle!

Examples: 30°, 45°, 60° and 75°

Real life objects: the tip of a sharpened pencil, a slice of pizza, clock hands at 2 o'clock, and the pointed roof of a house!

Module 2

Obtuse Angles

Select an angle below then click Let's Draw!

Hello Explorer! 👋

An Obtuse angle is any angle greater than 90° but less than 180°. It is wider and more open than a right angle!

Examples: 100°, 120°, 135° and 150°

Real life objects: an open book lying flat, a reclining chair, clock hands at 8 o'clock, and the wings of a butterfly when fully spread!

Module 3

Reflex Angles

Select an angle below then click Let's Draw!

Hello Explorer! 👋

A Reflex angle is any angle greater than 180° but less than 360°. It wraps all the way around past a straight line!

Examples: 200°, 240°, 270° and 300°

Real life objects: the wide opening of a folding fan, a clock hand sweeping the long way round, the inner curve of a boomerang, and the opening of a snail shell!
