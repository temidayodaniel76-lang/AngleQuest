# AngleQuest
**Discover the World of Angles!**

This interactive instructional material is designed specifically for **Grade 7 students**, making the study of angles simple, engaging, and enjoyable. Learners will explore the different types of angles, understand their properties, and develop confidence in accurately drawing and identifying them through guided practice.

To make learning even more exciting, the material includes **interactive mini-games** that reinforce key concepts, maintain learners' attention, and transform practice into a fun, rewarding experience. By combining clear explanations, hands-on activities, and game-based learning, this resource helps students build a strong foundation in geometry while staying motivated throughout their learning journey.
